If you are running behind

  1. 1–3 days behind: use the next Sunday review morning. Reschedule over 3–5 days.
  2. 4–7 days behind: reschedule over 7–10 days. In Phase 1, skip lectures for topics you already know well (crystallography, defects, phase diagrams). Do High yield topics first.
  3. More than a week behind: shorten Phase 2. Halve the topic-wise PYQ days for your strongest section, but keep every full mock.
  4. Last 10 days before the exam: no new topics. Only formula sheets, PYQs of weak topics, and mocks.

Rules

  • Never skip the practice block. Previous-year questions (PYQs) show how deep each topic is examined.
  • Read first, then watch. Only watch lectures for topics you couldn't follow, at 1.5–2× speed.
  • Write one formula page a day. By mid-December you should have one formula sheet per section.
  • When behind, catch up on Sunday morning. Never cut sleep.
  • When short of time, cut videos and low-yield reading, never mocks or PYQs.
  • New in the 2027 syllabus: silicon processing, Drude/Debye models, UV-Vis/IR/Raman. Pre-2026 XE-C papers don't cover them.
