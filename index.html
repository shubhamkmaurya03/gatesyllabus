Each row is a section of the official GATE 2027 XE syllabus (IIT Madras). Plan % fills automatically as you tick the plan tasks mapped to that section. Tick Confident only once you can solve its previous-year questions without notes.

Rules

  • Never skip the practice block. Previous-year questions (PYQs) show how deep each topic is examined.
  • Read first, then watch: only watch lectures on topics you could not follow, at 1.5–2× speed.
  • Write one formula page per day; by mid-December you should have one formula sheet per section.
  • If you fall behind, catch up on Sunday morning and use the catch-up planner. Never cut sleep.
  • If you're short of time, cut videos and low-yield reading, never mock tests or PYQs.
  • Check the 2027 syllabus changes: silicon processing, Drude/Debye models and UV-Vis/IR/Raman are new. Papers from before 2026 (XE-C) do not cover them.

If you are running behind

  1. Behind by 1–3 days: use the next Sunday review morning and the catch-up planner (spread over 3–5 days).
  2. Behind by 4–7 days: spread over 7–10 days. In Phase 1, skip lectures for topics you already know (crystallography, defects, phase diagrams are your home ground). Topics tagged HIGH YIELD come first.
  3. Behind by more than a week: shorten Phase 2. Cut topic-wise PYQ days for your strongest section to half, but keep every full mock test.
  4. Last 10 days before the exam: stop new topics. Only formula sheets, PYQs of weak topics and mocks.
